Case Studies

How I've made an impact

01
Deploying AI Contract Review Across a High-Volume Practice

At Liberty & Freedom Legal Group, the legal team was spending significant attorney time on first-pass review of regulatory filings, vendor agreements, and compliance documents across 25+ concurrent matters. I implemented a structured AI-assisted review workflow using Kira and Luminance, defining extraction templates for key clauses, obligation flags, and non-standard terms. The output was a standardized issue summary delivered to attorneys ready for review — reducing first-pass time and improving consistency across matter types.

Result Structured AI review workflow deployed across the full caseload. Attorney first-pass time materially reduced. Standardized summary format adopted team-wide for all incoming agreements.
02
Building Legal Operations Infrastructure from Scratch

At Thompson, Brody & Kaplan LLP, recurring drafting workflows were ad hoc — no reusable templates, no standardized checklists, no centralized matter tracking. I built a document automation library using Contract Express and HotDocs, covering the most frequently used agreement types. I also introduced a Jira-based matter status system that gave partners real-time visibility into a 30+ matter caseload and replaced the weekly manual reporting process with a structured dashboard updated by the team.

Result Reusable template library across core agreement types. Matter tracker eliminating manual status reporting. Consistent billing and deadline monitoring framework implemented across the practice.
